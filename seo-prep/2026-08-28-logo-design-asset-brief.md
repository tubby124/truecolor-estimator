## Page story

Lead with logo design because that is the phrase customers recognize. Prove that True Color can
turn the logo into usable print artwork for signs, cards, flyers, banners, decals, magnets, wall
graphics and displays. The visual story should be `idea -> clean logo system -> real printed use`,
not a generic designer-at-a-laptop stock page.

## Generation and selection rules

- Use original or customer-approved work only. Do not imitate an existing brand or display a
  client logo without permission.
- Keep text and logo geometry clean enough to survive close inspection. Reject warped lettering,
  gibberish, broken perspective, fake printer controls and malformed hands.
- Show the same identity across several applications where possible. Consistency proves the
  `design + print in one shop` advantage better than six unrelated mockups.
- Match True Color's customer-facing palette: espresso/black, warm cream and cyan accents. The
  portfolio artwork itself can use other colours.
- Leave negative space for page headings and mobile crops. Do not bake pricing, CTAs or long copy
  into the image.
- Export WebP in sRGB. Aim for less than 250 KB for each 1200x900 image and less than 350 KB for
  the hero without visible compression damage.

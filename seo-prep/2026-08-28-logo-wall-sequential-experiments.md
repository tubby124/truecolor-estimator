# Logo Design and Wall Graphics sequential experiment plan

Prepared: 2026-08-28  
Status: staged only; no Logo Design or Wall Graphics page mutation is authorized by this file.

## Why both experiments are held

The Flyer Printing metadata experiment shipped on 2026-08-23, while finalized GSC data is
currently available only through 2026-08-24. That is about one finalized post-release day, not
the required 7–14 completed days. The site therefore has no room for a second live SEO-page
experiment yet.

No new location page is proposed. Both experiments retain their existing URLs and canonicals.

## Experiment 1 — Logo Design on the existing Graphic Design page

Run this first only after the Flyer observation gate closes. Keep the canonical URL
`/graphic-design-saskatoon`; the page should lead with the phrase customers recognize while
retaining the wider graphic-design service vocabulary.

Proposed first release scope:

- Title: `Logo Design Saskatoon | In-House Design & Print | True Color`
- Meta description: `Logo design in Saskatoon for new logos, vector cleanup and print-ready artwork. Design + print in one shop. Standard design work is $40 flat.`
- Open Graph title and description: match the title and meta description.
- H1: `Logo Design in Saskatoon`
- Subtitle: `New logos, vector cleanup and print-ready artwork—plus graphic design for the signs, cards, flyers and displays you need next.`
- Opening: explain that the logo is built to work on signs, cards, banners, decals and wall
  graphics, then name vector cleanup, resizing and print-ready layouts.
- Supporting H2: `Graphic design beyond the logo`, followed by the existing evidence-backed
  services for banners, signs, flyers, cards, vehicle magnets and display layouts.

Price wording must stay internally consistent: standard design work is $40 flat; complex
multi-product, vehicle and custom brand-system work is quoted after the brief is reviewed. Do not
promise that every original logo or every scope is $40.

Keep URL, canonical, estimator destination, service breadth and internal product links unchanged.
Do not bundle FAQ/schema expansion or a URL redirect into the first experiment.

## Experiment 2 — Wall Graphics metadata

Run only after Experiment 1 has completed its own 7–14 finalized-day observation window and a
fresh page/query rebaseline confirms the Wall Graphics click signal has not persisted.

Proposed title-only candidate:

`Wall Graphics Saskatoon | Removable Wall Vinyl from $11/sqft`

The first release should be metadata-only. Do not bundle body, H1, FAQ, schema or URL changes.
Separately correct the page's inaccurate `Roland UV` equipment wording to the verified
eco-solvent printer/cutter description, but do not combine that factual cleanup with the CTR
experiment.

## Release gates for each experiment

1. Verify the preceding experiment's production title, canonical, indexability and sitemap date.
2. Collect 7–14 completed finalized GSC days plus GA4 landing-page context.
3. Compare page and query impressions, clicks, CTR and position against the pre-release baseline.
4. Keep, revise or revert before opening the next experiment.
5. Log the exact files, deploy date, baseline, decision date and result in `memory/seo-sprints.md`.

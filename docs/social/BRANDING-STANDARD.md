## September 6 historical direction and conditional logo workflow

The following preserves the September 6 review checkpoint, not current queue or deployment evidence. Apply the preparation workflow only when the selected recipe calls for a logo and the current scoped direction permits it.

Owner confirmed September 6, 2026: use the existing official True Color logo in a sensible corner to establish the brand consistently. The first white-footer treatment was rejected after preview: the mark should be transparent and fit naturally into the composition. The owner then selected the transparent wordmark direction and requested TRUECOLOR on the first line and PRINTING.CA beneath it, making the website readable in the mark. The owner accepted the new logo look. The 24-creative storyboard is prepared for separate month review; the new variant is not yet deployed as the main company logo.

Preserve `public/truecolorlogo.png` unchanged. The private candidate retains its distinctive upper lettering and colours, typesets PRINTING.CA beneath it, and removes the background. Place a transparent derivative in genuine empty space with a consistent inset. Choose the corner per image; never force a mark onto the product. Use dark lettering on light backgrounds and light lettering on dark backgrounds, retaining the colour accents. Start around 20% of the image width with a 3.5% inset; inspect each placement. Preserve the whole source image; never cover customer logos, printed text, QR codes or product edges. Do not ask an image model to redraw the logo or customer artwork. Resizing and JPEG encoding can change pixels, but must not change the depicted content.

Create a new versioned derivative during preparation, before final media review and approval. Keep the original, source/logo/output hashes and layout metadata privately. The reviewed uploaded JPEG is the approved asset. Never apply branding at dispatch time to an already approved file, and never silently rebrand existing ready or posted deliveries. If a source already includes the mark, use its recorded derivative rather than branding it again.

The preparation helper `scripts/social/brand-media.mjs` requires an explicit transparent logo and reviewed corner. It does not infer a safe placement. This is not an automatic watermark on every application upload. A future hosted worker using a logo recipe must call the same composition step before presenting its output for approval; no-logo recipes skip it. Other businesses need their own verified logo and business configuration; the True Color mark is not a default for other clients.

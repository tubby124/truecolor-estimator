Preserve `public/truecolorlogo.png` unchanged. The private candidate retains its distinctive upper lettering and colours, typesets PRINTING.CA beneath it, and removes the background. Place a transparent derivative in genuine empty space with a consistent inset. Choose the corner per image; never force a mark onto the product. Preserve the whole source image; never cover customer logos, printed text, QR codes or product edges. Do not ask an image model to redraw the logo or customer artwork. If a source already includes the mark, use its recorded derivative rather than branding it again.

The preparation helper `scripts/social/brand-media.mjs` requires an explicit transparent logo and reviewed corner. It does not infer a safe placement. This is not an automatic watermark on every application upload. A future hosted worker must call the same composition step before presenting its output for approval. Other businesses need their own verified logo and business configuration; the True Color mark is not a default for other clients.

## Repeatable routing

1. Inspect source completeness, readability, useful context, identity and permissions. Search existing originals before asking for recapture.
2. **KEEP** when already clean or when process/installation context matters. **REVISE** when background cleanup helps and the complete product can be preserved. **HOLD / RESELECT** when cropped, obscured, unclear or mismatched to its label.
3. Preserve the original; save a separate candidate with parent/output hashes, prompt/tool/recipe version, reason and observed usage. Do not generate missing text, codes or product edges.
4. Independently compare full-size critical details and phone composition. Record observed defects separately from unverified properties. Recheck corrections; retain the original as fallback.
5. Review final image, caption, destination and time together. Changed media must not silently replace an approved rendition.

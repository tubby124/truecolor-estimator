# Product gallery and social harness checkpoint

September 9, 2026. This is a sanitized implementation record. Private source imagery, customer artwork, crop records, hashes, review exports, account data, and publication state remain outside this public repository.

## Owner-approved direction

The frequent product-showcase post is photo-first: a full 4:5 product image with one small, exact True Color mark in a reviewed non-product corner. It carries no repeated footer, phone/address strip, headline, price, or product label. The caption supplies the product context and one appropriate action.

This is the default **gallery showcase** lane, not a universal layout rule:

- **Offer:** a distinct, type-led treatment may show one current, configuration-bound price or promotion. It must resolve from the maintained pricing source at preparation time and preserve its qualifiers.
- **Seasonal/campaign:** a time-bound, audience-specific treatment may use a stronger visual concept after product, calendar window, claims, and owner review are set.
- **Customer work:** authentic customer results remain a separate evidence lane. Illustrative product images must never be represented as customer work, and customer artwork must not be reused without its recorded permission/context.

## Product-gallery target

The site target is a complete clean product gallery: each scoped product gets intentional overview, material/detail, application, and alternate views where the product recipe calls for them. Material/product evidence should show useful differences—for example, coroplast flute/edge construction, different real-use settings, or vehicle-decals application contexts—rather than four cosmetic variations of one image.

The current private inventory is 75 new gallery sources plus 6 retained overview sources: 81 effective sources of 124 planned. The remaining 43 are planned work, not missing live website assets. New generation is owner-controlled; no automatic batch resumes merely because a queue exists.

## SEO-safe replacement gate

No image is swapped into a product or SEO route until its mapping record identifies the old placement, target page, product/variation, source/derivative status, alt text, dimensions, and permission/provenance state. Retain customer-work placements until they have an explicit, separate disposition.

The authoritative regression-prevention procedure is the pending [gallery assurance runbook (PR #73)](https://github.com/tubby124/truecolor-estimator/pull/73). It supplies the protected-route inventory, pre/post invariants, source/consumer proof, scoped rollback, image sitemap/Merchant gates, and current-evidence limitations. This harness checkpoint does not duplicate or relax those rules.

For each approved replacement:

1. Preserve route URLs, headings, structured-data entities, internal links, and page intent; a media change is not authorization for an SEO rewrite.
2. Use descriptive, lowercase-hyphenated filenames and natural 10–125 character alt text that identifies the visible product/material/use. Do not keyword-stuff or use a filename as alt text.
3. Deliver appropriate responsive derivatives with intrinsic dimensions to prevent layout shift. Below-fold gallery images may lazy-load and decode asynchronously; do not lazy-load the page's LCP/hero image.
4. Prefer modern delivery formats with compatible fallbacks where the existing application supports them. Verify rendered image URLs, `srcset`/`sizes`, width/height, accessibility text, and actual page placement before promotion.
5. Keep image sitemap, Merchant, GBP, paid, email, and social distribution behind the existing rights/provenance gates. A local asset or successful build does not establish permission, indexing, or external distribution.

## Social copy and planning gate

The private copy system has caption templates, local/product/audience hashtag pools, and a per-post record. Final product-specific captions, offer records, seasonal recipes, and the year calendar remain to be built.

Every prepared post needs: selected source/crop, lane, caption, selected hashtags, CTA/contact choice, verified offer binding when priced, owner approval, and separately recorded scheduled/published status. Preparation does not schedule or publish.

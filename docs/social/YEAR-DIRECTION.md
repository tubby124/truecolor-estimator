# Rolling year direction — October 2026 to September 2027

Owner-approved rhythm: daily cleared real customer work plus three creative posts per week. Monthly review and feedback precede each exact release. The themes below are proposed starting points, not an approved batch or posting authorization. Daily real-work supply is checked each month; shortages remain visible instead of inventing jobs.

| Month | Creative direction for monthly review |
| --- | --- |
| October | Shop windows, autumn events and seasonal packaging; Halloween artwork where relevant. |
| November | Winter storefront preparation, gift packaging, cards and event displays. |
| December | A coordinated two-week holiday sequence: varied windows, labels, cards and gift-oriented scenes; transition into New Year. Avoid repeating one scene. |
| January | New-year business refresh: cards, signage and practical artwork help. Continue the holiday transition only where useful. |
| February | Seasonal gift labels, warm retail displays and occasion-specific designs. |
| March | Spring preparation, refreshed windows and event materials. |
| April | Outdoor sign planning and spring retail graphics; verify movable holiday dates if selected. |
| May | Community events, markets, banners and portable displays. |
| June | Summer events, outdoor visibility and locally relevant seasonal designs. |
| July | Summer markets and Canada Day themes where suitable; avoid invented event affiliations. |
| August | Autumn preparation, education/event print and business refresh. |
| September | Fall storefronts, product packaging and planning the next seasonal cycle. |

## Monthly cycle

1. Review previous owner feedback and available engagement/delivery observations.
2. Select next month's actual cleared customer assets and creative themes; check inventory and repetition across both streams.
3. Prepare final renditions, captions with varied relevant facts, channel-specific copy and local times.
4. Present one complete monthly review. Capture requested changes against the affected image/caption/recipe.
5. Approve exact revised packages and enroll them through the existing queue.
6. Collect results and save versioned lessons for the following month. Keep missing metrics explicit; sales attribution is outside this milestone.

The annual outline gives continuity; it does not lock twelve months of prices or prevent improvements. Recipe changes affect new drafts. Changes to approved content require a newly reviewed version. A recurring reminder/worker is a separate setup step and has not been activated.
